Dawnguard is the capital city of the human-occupied territories of [[Solva]]. 

Dawnguard was built onto the southwestern shores of Solva as a shrine to [[Solaris]], due west of the southernmost mountains of the Ashen Peaks. Dawnguard was later built up and fortified as a strategic hold in the human struggle against the high elves of the Peaks, and has grown into the trading and cultural epicenter of human-Solva. 
## Founding
Dawnguard was built up from its humble farmland roots in the midst of the religious fervor spurred on by [[Tangmazu, the trickster]]. Once Tangmazu twisted the story of the Dancing Sisters into one of Warring sisters, humans quickly picked a side and worshiped her. Spiral Castle, the centerpiece of Dawnguard, was immediately built up, "to reach [[Solaris]]' height, to elevate her."

For centuries following, human Solvans began to look fondly upon the high elves' territory, and endeavored to snag their mountains for themselves; an exploit further fueled by modern rumors of rare earth materials underground. In this endeavor, Dawnguard evolved from a religious, cultural hub into a devastating war machine whose citizens were sent roaring into the Peaks.
## Social structure
Dawnguard is under a monarchy, and is presently (circa: AFTERSHOCK) ruled under Queen Lumsden. The crown's advisors, adjudicators, and friends of the crown are all empowered to wield the crown as a political weapon in their own right, but all answer to the crown. Dawnguard's generals are expected to handle her armies; Dawnguard's armies are expected to listen to the crown above their generals.

Beyond the crown, is a city full of chivalrous life, certain that their way of life is the cultural best. Within Dawnguard, the arts flourish, with many an artist being regularly commissioned to depict various knights' combat stories in song and paint. The downtrodden and less-fortunate of Dawnguard are often treated with respect, and encouraged to become a devout worshipper of Solaris.
## Notable locations:
### Spiral Castle
Spiral Castle is a monumental stone spire, built in a conical-horn shape. The base has a diameter of 600 ft., and the eponymous spiral stretches upwards of 2000 ft. A staircase in Spiral's center reaches ~1800 ft. high, leading to a small exit, leaving any who wish to reach the peak to climb the remaining 200 ft. on their own. Spiral Castle is the centerpiece of Dawnguard, hosting the monarch, their governing body, and a vast gathering area surrounded by food stands, buskers, and the passerby. All roads lead to Spiral, making it the single most populace intersection for foot traffic.
### Lupa Lumula
Lupa Lumula is an enclave of ethnic Wraeclastians within [[Dawnguard]]. Lupa Lumula was formed shortly after Wraeclast's sinking into the sea. The residents of Lupa Lumula are formed primarily those who wished for a better life outside of the clutches of [[Oriath]]'s stone walls and armored streets; it is named for a revolutionary who died standing against the Ebony Legion. 

While all Wraeclastians who washed up in Dawnguard stay close to Lupa Lumula, the culture in the conclave expressly rejects the governing bodies that ruled over them while in Wraeclast. Lupa Lumulans are Wraeclastian, rarely Oriathian.

After a terse few exchanges, and an abyssal fight, Arthas and company were returned to Spiral Castle unharmed -- with a new blade, as well.
