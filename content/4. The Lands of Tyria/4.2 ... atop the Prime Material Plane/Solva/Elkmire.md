Elkmire is a heavily fortified keep ran occupied the high elves in the [[Ashen Peaks]]. 
## Structure and layout
Elkmire is packed to the gills with an incredibly dense, multi-layered network of mass-meditation sites, smithies, craft shops, cookeries, mage studies, law enforcement and mass-surveillance, armories, counsel chambers, and other high security facilities. 

Elkmire defensively exploits Dawnguardians' inability to survive in cold climates. From its sheer, arched walls, to its denizens ability to rapidly retaliate from any direction of attack, Elkmire has become notorious in Dawnguardian circles as being impregnable and unconquerable.
## Societal structures
The strictly regimented architecture in Elkmire reflects the suffocating society that lives within it. Any high elf not willing to hone their craft, to physically train to failure, or to commit themselves wholly to the high elves' cause, is rejected from Elkmire. Elkmire maintains a culture of communal militarism by reminding its citizens of [[Dawnguard|the threats it faces from below]], and demanding that each elf work to shield the Ashen Peaks from them.
## Notable locations
Deep within the Ashen Peaks below Elkmire, a series of prison cells exists. It was excavated into the mountain shortly after Dawnguard launched their first series of attacks on the high elves, and serves to house prisoners taken from Dawnguard's front lines. Prisoners who end up in here serve as sponges for the high elves' hatred, and are made to endure untold amounts of physical and mental torture and exploitation. Mages often visit this mountain prison to experiment on a living subject.
## History
Following the [[Solstice]], Elkmire saw no change in its operation; no beast bothers Elkmire.
