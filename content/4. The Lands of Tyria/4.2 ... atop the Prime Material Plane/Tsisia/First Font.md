The First Font is a wide, loosely pressurized opening to the [[Plane of Water]]. It supplies water to [[Lake Taymyr]], and as of the [[Solstice]], is one of the two intentional openings into the [[Plane of Water]].
## Hydrology and hydraulics
The First Font propels ~5,000,000 cubic feet of water into Lake Taymyr per second. 

In its initial form, the diameter of the First Font was 160 feet, and the surge of water flowed at a velocity of 250 feet per second (170 mph). After its widening, the Font expanded to 2700 feet across, and the resulting surge of water slowed to .87 feet per second (.6 mph)
## History
[[Torentia]] opened the First Font shortly after the [[Divinarius]] died.

Following the [[Solstice]], the First Font opened wider, enabling casual traversal through the Font. This widening connected the Plane of Water to Lake Taymyr, enabling Tyria-borne creatures' travel into the Plane of Water, and vice versa.
