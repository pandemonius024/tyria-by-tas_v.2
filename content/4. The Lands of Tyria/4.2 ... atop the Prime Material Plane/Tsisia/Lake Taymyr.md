Lake Taymyr is a body of water which [[Tsisia]] encapsulates. Lake Taymyr contains the [[First Font]], and feeds [[Torrent's Reign]].
## Hydrology and hydraulics
Lake Taymyr is loosely in the shape of an oblong pyramid; it is 180 miles across its longer length, and 95 miles across its shorter length. Lake Taymyr contains ~900 cubic miles of water. At its deepest point, Lake Taymyr connects to the [[Plane of Water]] through the First Font.
## History
Following the [[Solstice]], Lake Taymyr and the Plane of Water became, hydrologically, one contiguous body. This caused Lake Taymyr to become dominated by numerous, varied, multicolored coral reefs, and equally varied coral reef fish. This coral boom is somewhat responsible for the equivalent boom in amphibious life.
