Tyria's Root is a realm-shaping tree whose roots stretch far into the crust of the Prime Material Plane, and is thought to be the origin of all woodland life across Tyria. Tyria's Root stands at over 1,200 meters tall, with a diameter of 200 meters at its base. It is large enough to house a society of elves inside its trunk, who labor to protect the health of Tyria's Root and the surrounding forest.
## Structure and layout
Tyria's Root acts as a communal center for the wood elves who live in and around it. The Root has a long series of floors carved out of its interior, with a hollow central tunnel extending from the ground floor past the canopy layer. Tyria's Root bears more resemblance to a massive, hollowed-out tree trunk than a big, living tree.

The roots of the Root are similarly hollowed out, and capable of allowing single-file lines of wood elves to traverse them. This underground-wooden cave network has enabled the wood elves to defend their territory from invaders, most notably those from Asteria.

Tyria's Root is enclosed on all sides, except for the canopy. Entering the Root requires passing between or through the wooden facade, or otherwise flying in from the top.
## History
Following ASCENSION, Tyria's Root is found to be plagued by a strange, purple fungi that forcibly controls the bodies of the elves it seeds upon. This fungi was then burnt out by [[Booke]] and company, liberating the elves, and healing the [[Forsaken Forest]].

In the Eclipsed timeline, Tyria's Root is poisoned by minions of [[Chaos]], who arrive at the tree from a portal to the Realm of Chaos. The SOULREND party deign to close it, fighting their way to the very top of the tree, allowing [[Eliza Khione]] seal the rift shut with Lunaris' moonstone. 

In the Solsticed timeline, Tyria's Root remains perfectly healthy, and the elves went right to work repairing the damages that the fungi had caused. 

Post-Solstice, the elves of Tyria's Root were temporarily forced into the Root by Shadowfallen creatures and Chaotic wildlife, alike. While the modern elves of the Root have managed to strike an accord with the Chaotic wildlife, the Shadowfallen creatures that plague the Forsaken Forest remain a violent threat for the Root to contend with.
