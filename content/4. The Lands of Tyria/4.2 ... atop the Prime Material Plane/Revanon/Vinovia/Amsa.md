Amsa is a large, centrally powered mechanical city whose 6 towering legs carry it across [[Vinovia]]. It is surrounded at all times by a sandy mirage, which renders the city invisible to onlookers standing more than a quarter mile away. Compasses that have been specifically tuned to Amsa's aetheric signature can be used to navigate to Amsa from beyond this mirage. 
## Getting to Amsa
Amsan compasses are tuned to the aetheric signature of the heart of [[Etris]], a source of raw magic harvested from the ex-god of wind. Using the heart of Etris, Amsa's sole remaining creator, [[Pye, the begotten]], can navigate the sands at will. Compasses are relatively cheap to make, but need to be tuned while in Amsa -- preferably, as close to the core as possible. A compass cannot be made outside of Amsa. This means that compasses are a lifeline back to Amsa, and are typically guarded closely, like one's coin purse.

Ferry services to Amsa operate out of [[La Escuela de la Montanha]] and [[Ipomoea]]. These ferries offer safe passage into Amsa at a steep price. These ferrymen are often operated by the casinos, cartels, manufacturers and industrialists, and any other group that has an interest in pulling more bodies into Amsa. 
## Amsa's creation and founding
Amsa was originally made by: the patron of knowledge, [[Cassandra Lumen]]; an enigmatic Dhampir artificer, Pye; and ex-anthropologist Aviria. Amsa was a walking hovel used as the team's base-camp during their desert-y explorations. In the beginning, it was no larger than a square rug, with a bare wooden surface for resting, and a sizeable amount of storage fitted underneath.

As the number of the team's discoveries increased*, so too did their team size. As more archaeologists and anthropologists insisted on tagging along with the researchers, the need for more carrying capacity grew. Pye became the foremost engineer dedicated to expanding Amsa's capacity, marking the start of a lifelong project: the growth and maintenance of Amsa.

> \*(Many of this teams' archaeological discoveries ended up being chronicled in [[A segment from VINOVIA, by Emi R. Tas|VINOVIA]] by this compendium's author, [[Emi R. Tas]]!)

Many became enchanted by the walking camp. Stories were told of a walking, kneeling room that could support dozens atop its surface. Passerby met the fledgling Amsa with wonder and curiosity. It became a desert novelty, something to admire, and something to seek out.

This desire, both Pye's dedication to his craft, and the magnetic attraction it had to the otherwise-bored desert wanderers, fueled Amsa's growth. Pye became detached from his archaeological study, and focused on building bigger, better. When Aviria and company slew Etris, Pye claimed the heart to fuel a sophisticated hydraulic system that increased Amsa's carrying capacity by several orders of magnitude. For decades since [[Hasha]] fell in 12 AS, Pye fashioned a larger, and larger Amsa, until he finally attracted enough desert wanderers as mechanically-minded as he to help him. Pye and a team of engineers continue to maintain and grow Amsa to this day.
## Structure and organization
Amsa is a ~200 acre machine that hobbles on 6 legs, with a hexagonal footprint of ~1/2 acre. The legs are divided into 2 groups of 3 on either side of Amsa, and produces a relatively stable gait by alternating stepping legs. Steps land in intervals of 15 seconds, and produce a deep thudding sound and light shudders throughout the city. A suspension system dulls the stepping reverberations before they reach topside.

The city is a layer cake of platforms, scaffolding, and staircases, built atop a steel chassis connected to the leg mechanisms. There are 2 distinct regions in Amsa: Amsa's topside, and Amsa's underside. Topside Amsa can typically see [[Solaris|the sun]], breathe sandy air, and is situated where the original archaeological team would rest. Underside Amsa is made to breathe in its dust, do battle with [[Shadowfell|Shadowfallen]] creatures spewing from its maze of machinery, and live among lubricated pistons and bone-crunching gears.

Since the city may be balanced on 3 legs at any given moment, it is pivotal that no one side or corner of Amsa is heavier than the others. This building restriction has resulted in an Amsa that is circularly symmetric in weight distribution, but not necessarily symmetric architecturally. Warehouses of bastilite underside may be counterbalanced by an entire wall of casinos, brothels, and housing topside. Any building proposal goes directly through Pye, who is primarily concerned with Amsa's weight distribution.
## Societal structures
Amsa's isolated nature makes it an ideal city of vice, and its disconnect from Revanon's greater society makes it a safe haven for all sorts of unsavory folk. 

The physical divide between topside and underside Amsa corresponds to a social divide between the two sets of citizens. Topside Amsa is largely populated by eccentric artificers; shopkeepers peddling mechanical-technological wares; casino operators and high-rollers; elites enjoying a desert vacation; automation engineers; and other particularly wealthy citizens. Underside Amsa is largely populated by the mass of workers who've been tasked with keeping the place running; refugees, once seeking safe harbor, now with no place to turn; drug, gambling addicts; people lauded with steep debts to any number of Amsa's criminal underbelly; automation engineers; and Amsa's criminal underbelly. 

Amsa, politically, was divided into 5 parts: allegiance to [[Eternos]], the [[Outfit]], the [[Weave]], the local Thieves' Guild chapter, and Amsa's government itself. Citizens in underside Amsa were forced, in one way or another, to participate in 1 of the 4 gangs, for protection, for money, or for safety. Citizens in topside Amsa were largely ushered under the wing of Amsa's official civil guard, who labor to upkeep Amsa's status quo.

Following the Weave's destruction of the Outfit in the course of ETERNITY, and Dante's bombing of Eternos' HQ (and the surrounding area) during SOLSTICE, the power structures within underside Amsa have all but fallen to bits. In response, the Amsan civil guard has ramped up its efforts in quashing the underside, and begun anxiously policing her citizens.

Some underside have taken up arms in attempts to re-conquer Amsa's underbelly. Nero du Fleur-Donati gathered the remains of the Weave into [[Arachnis]], a revival sect of the Weave currently busy with recruitments, establishing ties into the civil guard, and settling territorial lines by making friends of the locale. Jaxon Jet of Eternos is leading similar efforts. Further, influence from the Shadowfell has spread into Amsa's legs post-Solstice, forcing Amsa's civil guard to maintain a heightened military presence at each leg juncture. Lastly, the [[Unseen]] have found it prudent to gain power in Amsa; Ambrose's successor, Five, has made it a point to stake a claim to the walking city. Five has moved to coerce the richest operators in Amsa, towards the ends of exploiting the flow of capital in order to indirectly manage Amsans. 

Following the [[Solstice]], Amsa has become a battleground for more than just creatures. The [[Realm of Chaos]] has taken a liking to topside's love of gambling, freeform lovemaking, and spontaneous drive towards progress. Meanwhile, underside has been subsumed by the Shadowfell's influence, drawn to undersiders' dog-eat-dog mentality, as well as Amsa's brutally organized machinery.
## Notable locations:
### Corcoran Laboratory
Corcoran Laboratory is situated in topside Amsa, and focuses its efforts on the research and development of genetic mutations, and evolutionary branching. Corcoran Laboratory was temporarily taken by force by [[Tangmazu, the trickster]], in the lead-up to SOLSTICE with the explicit goal of developing bio-weapons capable of dominating the world. 

Following the SOLSTICE campaign, Corcoran Laboratory was liberated from Tangmazu's influence, and business as usual resumed.
### Green Bay Plaza
Green Bay Plaza is a square constructed on topside Amsa, an uncharacteristically clean and formal area of the walking city. The Plaza is a horseshoe-shaped rink, with food stands, entertainment, and all sorts of party-enabling accoutrement. The hole in the center is rimmed with an automated anti-gravity net, allowing Plaza visitors to leap off into the desert, and be whisked safely back to where they leapt. 

The Plaza is surrounded on all sides by aggressive fans and air conditioning, covered in flowing silks and generous awnings, and provides denizens with a sumptuous view of the desert.
