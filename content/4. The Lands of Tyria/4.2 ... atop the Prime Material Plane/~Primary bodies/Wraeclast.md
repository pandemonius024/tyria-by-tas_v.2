Wraeclast was a continent in the [[Material Plane]], having lost its continental status after being sunk into the water by [[Torentia]]. 

Wraeclast was initially made of the refuse [[Tyria]] discarded in the making of the [[Divinarius]]. 
## Sinking into the Sea
Torentia was motivated to destroy the continent of Wraeclast after signs of [[Kitava, the drowned]]'s immediate return became apparent. In doing so, Kitava and his supporters have been drowned away, leaving nothing but a handful of disparate islands to dot the seascape that was once Wraeclast.
