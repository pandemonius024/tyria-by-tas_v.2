Tsisia is a continent located far to the north of [[Revanon]], beyond [[Torrent's Reign]]. 

Tsisia rests directly atop [[Tyria]]'s left clavicle and left trapezius. Tsisia surrounds [[Lake Taymyr]], and is itself surrounded by the great expanse of outer space. At the center of Tsisia is the [[First Font]], which is an opening to the [[Plane of Water]] and is the source of the Unending Sea.
## Ecology
Tsisia is primarily occupied by amphibious creatures who are capable of seeking shelter atop Tsisia, and hunting for food in Lake Taymyr. The continent has been untouched by the [[Divinarius|3 divine ancestries]] since its creation. 

Following the [[Solstice]], Tsisia was tinged heavily with the [[Realm of Chaos]], causing countless amphibians to be struck with heightened consciousness and other random mutations. Creatures who have gained consciousness consider themselves Tsisians, generally do not consider creatures without consciousness to be Tsisians.

A few patterns in these mutations emerged. Crocodiles and wyverns were granted the ability to stand upright and wield tools, and promptly began to arm themselves with weapons to fight over land. Beavers answered their instinctual call on a greater scale, fashioning expansive water works projects, which laid the foundation for a larger, more sedentary society. Newts developed a keen sense for the [[Luminiferous Aether]], and began experimenting with it in earnest, developing magic of their own. 
## A budding society
In the decades following the Solstice, Tsisia has evolved into a loose network of battling tribes. 

A growing hegemon of large-bodied crocodilefolk has been settling in the east, atop Tyria's left shoulder; beaverfolk have found themselves busy with a generational civil project to dam Torrent's Reign. Salamanderfolk and newtfolk have been scattered across Tsisia, experimenting with their newfound arcane-adaptation, while frogfolk and toadfolk have been busy enjoying their lives. Many snakes have evolved wings, and have simply left for the [[Plane of Air]] above.

40 years into achieving sentience, Tsisians, by and large, have resisted developing any monolithic empires. Much like the 3 divine ancestries below, Tsisians have struggled for dominance over each other, whether dominance be sought in the glory of combat, or in the intellectual battleground of mega-dam construction planning. Tsisia teems with more life than it ever has before, both above and below the water; for that fact, it is more chaotic, more incendiary, and more lush with beauty than ever before.
