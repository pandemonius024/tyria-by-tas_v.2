Torrent's Reign is a 400+ mile tall waterfall beginning in [[Lake Taymyr]] and ending far north of [[Revanon]]. Torrent's Reign spills over [[Tyria]]'s left clavicle, pours down her sternum, and terminates in the [[Unending Sea]].
## Hydrology and hydraulics
Torrent's Reign dumps over 4,300,000 cubic feet of water per second over Tsisia and into the Unending Sea. This flow is in free fall for the first ~40 miles, reaching a peak velocity of 98 feet per second (68 mph) before contacting on Tyria, where it then is channeled down Tyria's sternum at a decreasingly acute slope.

The flow regime of Torrent's Reign is supercritical as it flows down Tyria's sternum, with an average velocity of 40 feet per second (27 mph) midway down. When Torrent's Reign enters the Unending Sea, the resulting hydraulic jump creates a depth difference of an average of 8 feet, up to a maximum of ~14 feet at the center of the flow.
