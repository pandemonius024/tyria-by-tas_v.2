The Luminiferous Aether is the fabric of reality upon which all planes, demi or otherwise, exist upon. It is the medium through which aetheric effects propagate. 
## Properties
The Aether is produced by the [[Maelström]], and observably flows away from the center of the Maelström. The Aether is present in every corner of reality, except for the [[Void]], where the Luminiferous Aether is shred to nothing.

The Aether is uninteractable through non-aetheric means. The Aether does not appear to react to the movement of non-aetherically charged objects, even if said objects produce aetheric or supernatural effects. The aether will react to objects made of [[Bastilite]], scrolls which contain spells, trinkets which contain spells, or particularly powerful foci. These objects warp the Aether around them, in a similar manner to how magnets warp the magnetic field.

Spells travel through the Aether at a speed inversely proportional to the effect of the spell. For instance, spells that change the visible color of [[Lunaris]] will create that change with more rapidity than spells that aim to physically pull Lunaris to Tyria. The fabrication of a dining hall complete with a feast takes longer to appear than the fabrication of a feast.
## Discovery
Aether theory was first postulated by the Observer Queiedie of [[Dweomerheart]], who found that they were able to telepathically move stones and other objects through space, despite those stones not making any sound when knocked together. This suggested to Queiedie that their telepathic intent was being transmitted through a medium altogether unique from our physical atmosphere, which is the medium that sound would have needed to travel through. 

Queiedie first suggested the existence of the aether in the Seventh Book of Opticks:

> Doth not this aethereal medium in passing out of water, glass, crystal, and other compact and dense bodies in empty spaces, grow denser and denser by degrees, and by that means refract the rays of magic not in a point, but by bending them gradually in curve lines? ...Is not this medium much rarer within the dense bodies of [[Solaris]], the stars, planets and comets, than in the empty celestial space between them? And in passing from them to great distances, doth it not grow denser and denser perpetually, and thereby cause the gravity of those great bodies towards one another, and of their parts towards the bodies; every body endeavouring to go from the denser parts of the medium towards the rarer?

The Aether was later substantiated by the Observer Faciendum. In 416 AS, Faciendum found a basis for the Aether during an experiment later dubbed the "Michelson-Morley experiment". Two homunculi, which Faciendum had named Michelson and Morley respectively, were rigged to produce large flashes of colorful light at the same instant in time. When the homunculi produced the flashes of light when in motion, Faciendum observed a difference in how the flashes of light were interfering with one another as compared to how they interfered while stationary. This suggested to Faciendum that the initial velocity of the homunculi with respect to the stationary, intertial Aether mattered to the outcome of spells. The significance of this relative motion would inform later studies on Aetheric theory.

Faciendum later attempted to reinforce this claim by launching itself at extreme speeds across Dweomerheart while telepathically holding a stone at a constant, far away distance in front of it. Other Observers in attendance noted that the stone lagged behind where it should have been in relation to Faciendum, suggesting that there was a time lag between Faciendum's arcane intention, and the actual movement of the stone. This experiment substantiated Faciendum's earlier claim, and also sent them into an early retirement.
## Creation
It is theorized that the Luminiferous Aether was created by the [[Tear of Space]].
