The planes are a series of surfaces of varying stellar depth that comprise the universe. The [[Prime Material Plane]] is one of these planes.
## Stellar depth
A single plane can be explored in 3 dimensions: width, depth, and height. The planes co-exist across a 4th dimension: stellar depth. Stellar depth is a relational value that compares the positions of two planes. 

For example, the Plane of Flame has a low stellar depth with respect to the Plane of Earth; that means that the planes of Flame and Earth are relatively near one another. Conversely, the Plane of Flame has a larger stellar depth with respect to the Plane of Water; that means that the planes of Flame and Earth are closer together than the planes of Flame and Water.
## Traversal
It is possible to traverse stellar depth in a number of ways. Skilled spellcasters can will themselves and company into distant positions in distant planes: skilled climbers can climb a tree out of the Plane of Wood into the Plane of Water; swimming up far enough in the Plane of Water leads you into the Plane of Air. Generally, these sorts of physical traversals require a low stellar depth to perform.

In general, whenever applicable, landmarks on one plane correlate to landmarks on other planes. Traversing stellar depth without changing width, depth, or height will tend to place you at similar locations to the one you left behind. Planewalking from [[Asteria]] in the Prime Material Plane usually lands you near a proportionally well-populated city in the [[Boneyard]]. There is no strict relationship between one's 3-dimensional position in one plane to the other. Planewalking into the Prime Material Plane from a well-populated city in the Boneyard can land you near Asteria, or [[Dawnguard]], with equal odds.

## Notable connections between planes
It's possible to physically travel between certain planes, just by walking or climbing. The follow list compiles a few notable connections between the planes:
- The Maelström swirled endlessly beneath the Realm of Chaos, it now swirls within the Plane of Flame;
- Rivers of molten metal flow from the Plane of Flame into the Plane of Metal;
- Trees from the Plane of Wood root in the Plane of Earth;
- "Sea level" marks the boundary between the Plane of Water and the Plane of Air;
- One can fall down a rabbit hole in the Prime Material Plane and fall into the Boneyard;
- The rivers Acheron and Cocytus flow from the Boneyard into Alethia;
- The river Phlegethon flows from beneath the Palace of the Dead directly into the Maelström.
