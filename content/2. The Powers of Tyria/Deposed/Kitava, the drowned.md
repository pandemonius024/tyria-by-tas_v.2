Kitava is a creature whose sole aim is to consume all life. Kitava came into existence in celestial opposition to the unyielding hopefulness that [[Aris, the unwritten]] has come to embody. Kitava's hunger is boundless and insatiable. Kitava could not be satisfied by its brethren nor village, neither its house nor its people. 
## Appearance
Kitava is a building-sized humanoid bearing an "X" slashed onto its face. Its skin is charred black like a roast left turning over an open flame for weeks.
## History
Before humans began recording their history in text, Kitava was sealed away.

Prior to SUNDER, Kalarel, the Vile succeeded at undoing the seal by tempting Kitava with a gift: a tower constructed with corpses.

Following the [[Solstice]], [[Torentia]] sunk the continent of [[Wraeclast]] into the water, bringing Kitava into the Sea. Torentia then subdued Kitava by plugging its mouth with a spigot leading to the [[Plane of Water]]. 

In the Eclipsed timeline, Kitava is never sunk into the ocean, and is left to consume life on Wraeclast with wanton disregard.
