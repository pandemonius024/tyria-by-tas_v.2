The Divinarius were [[Tyria]]'s 3 children, molded out of clay at the dawn of time. Their corpses have come to rest atop Tyria's midriff, and now form the landmasses [[Revanon]], [[Basti]], and [[Solva]].
## The Divinarius in life
The 3 Divinarius were named Rah, Baht, and Shah; eldest to youngest. Tyria fashioned them each in her image and made for them a playground of rolling hills, devious adversaries, and amusing creatures. 

Rah was a boisterous, carefree ball of energy, always fiending for a playful head-to-head. Theirs was a life motivated by personal improvement, and of a love of competition. Baht was slender, and the most reserved of the Divinarius, having preferred to occupy themselves with Tyria and Tyria's world. Theirs was a world filled with wonder and delight, contented to be sat creating and appreciating. Shah liked to meet all of Rah's challenges head-on, and had a penchant for forming great tales of piracy and heroism from their antics. They were a high-minded and proud type, for certain.

For a time, they were content: theirs was a life of joy, of accidents, and of bandages. Tyria, ever watchful, labored to ensure that any harm they sustained would be swiftly addressed. Under her watchful eye and careful hand, the 3 Divinarius were emboldened to live as they wished. They were inseparable. Then, they grew older.

The Divinarius' bodies were not immortal, like Tyria's; they could not harbor life forever. No matter Tyria's efforts, no matter Baht's inventions or Rah or Shah's will, the Divinarius crumbled away. Their appendages and limbs went first, and their sentience went last. They died resting on Tyria's lap.

Tyria, in her grief, curled into a ball and cried over her children's corpses, shedding 3 tears: the [[Tear of Life]], the [[Tear of Space]], and the [[Tear of Time]]. 

The Tear of Life landed on Rah, and sprouted twin blades of grass that formed [[Solaris]] and [[Lunaris]]. The Dancing Sisters found joy in each other, and in them, Tyria found peace. The Tear of Space flew outwards from Tyria, forming the [[Luminiferous Aether]] and the great expanse of outer space, and in that space, Tyria found beauty. Last, the Tear of Time fell forward through time, dragging Tyria forward, and gave her world temporal momentum.
## The Divinarius' Offspring
The Divinarius were elemental creatures molded in Tyria's image; the three ancestries that arrived on the Divinarius were molded in the Divinarius' image. Rah, forming the continent Revanon, birthed the [[Dwarves]]; Baht, forming the continent Basti, birthed the [[Elves]]; Shah, forming the continent Solva, birthed the [[Humans]]. These 3 divine ancestries spread across those 3 divine continents, and in doing so, gave life back to them.
