Lucia was a tiefling member of the [[Unseen]]. Lucia was best known for her operations around the world alongside [[Rox]]. 
## History
During the REAVE campaign, Lucia slept with [[Qim Chie]] in an attempt to pry information about the party's involvement in [[Baveil]], to no avail.

After Rox became [[Lars]]' plaything at the epiphany, Lucia left Rox' side to return to her post under [[Ambrose Tenebris|Five]] in [[Asteria]].
