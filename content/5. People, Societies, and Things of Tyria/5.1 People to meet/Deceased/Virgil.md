Virgil was one of the [[Weave]]'s most inventive weapons creator, and had worked frequently alongside roommate and co-designer, [[Dante du Fleur]]. He was killed in [[Eternos]]' raid on the Weave's headquarters.

In life, Virgil was often found in one of two places: inside his study at the Weave's central headquarters, or loitering within his and Dante's apartment. Both locations housed his and Dante's projects, with the former product prototypes intended for the Weave, and the latter, personal projects.
## History
Early into the ETERNITY campaign, Virgil was kidnapped by [[Anton Essa]]'s goons at the behest of [[Tangmazu, the trickster]]. Virgil was considered by Anton's informants to be an "intellectual, whose mind deserved taking apart," and was ultimately used by Anton to learn more about the [[Tear of Time]].

At the end of ETERNITY's first arc, Virgil was rescued from Anton's tower, the Epiphany, by the party. 

Until his death near the end of SOLSTICE, Virgil remained close to the Weave's network of goons and shields. This habit put him directly in the line of fire during Eternos' decapitation raid.
